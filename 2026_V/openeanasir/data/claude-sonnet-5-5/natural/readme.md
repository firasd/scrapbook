**Translation**

**Obverse**

Say to Ea-nasir: thus (says) Nanni.

When you came here, you spoke to me as follows: "I will give Gimil-Sîn fine *gubāru* ingots." [Note 1] You left, but you did not do what you said. You set out *gubāru* ingots that were not fine before my messenger, and you said: "If you want to take them, take them; if you don't want to take them, go away!"

Who do you take me for, that you [. . .] [broken line] and treat me this way? I sent gentlemen, men of standing like yourself, to collect my purse, and you treated them with contempt. [Note 2] In hostile territory you sent them back to me empty-handed, once and twice.

**Reverse**

Among those who travel to Telmun, who has ever treated me like this? Yet you alone treated my messenger with contempt! [Over] the amount of silver that you take from my hand, you speak this way? [Note 3]

And on your account I paid 18 talents of copper to the palace, and Sumi-abum likewise paid 18 talents of copper. Apart from that, we also left a sealed tablet at the temple of Shamash.

As for that copper, see how you have treated me! You have kept my purse back in hostile territory. [Now] return my purse to me. [Note 4]

Know this: here I will not accept from you copper that is not good. In my courtyard I will inspect it [piece by piece?] and take it myself. [Note 5]

**Left edge**

And because you have treated me with contempt, I will make a formal rejection against you. [Note 6]

**Notes**

1. *gubāru* is of uncertain meaning here. I take it to be a kind of metal ingot, probably copper, but I have left it untranslated.
2. The verb *mêšu* ("treat with contempt, neglect") is my best interpretation of *tumēšam/temēšanni*. The broken line 17 (*ni-x-x-x-ma*) cannot be restored.
3. The sense of "ana mala kaspim ša ina qātiya tušēṣû" is uncertain. It may refer to silver Ea-nasir has already obtained from Nanni.
4. The sentence "kīsi ana qātiya šulumam elika išu" is obscure. It may mean "I have the full amount owing from you." I have translated only the clear demand to return the purse.
5. The line numbering jumps from 26 to 50, so an unknown amount of text may be missing between the two. The word *ištīna*, which I render "piece by piece", is uncertain, as is the verb *unassaq*.
6. The final clause is uncertain. I take it to mean that Nanni will reject the goods formally, and the translation of *nasiḫtam epuš-ka* is tentative.
