**Obverse**

1. To Ea-nasir
2. speak:
3. Thus (says) Nanni:
4. "Now, when you came,
5. you spoke thus to me:
6. 'Thus (says) you:
7. I will give fine ingots
8. to Gimil-Sin.'
9. You went off and you spoke to me (thus),
10. but you did not do it.
11. You set ingots that were not fine
12. before my messenger and said:
13. 'Thus (says) you:
14. If you want to take them, take them;
15. if you do not want to take them, go away!'
16. Who am I to you, that
17. you [have treated me so (?)] ... [damaged]
18. and send me word in this way?
19. I sent gentlemen like you
20. to collect my purse,
21. and you have shown them contempt,
22. [and] you have sent them back to me
23. in enemy territory,

**Reverse**

1. once and twice, with empty hands (lit. "their emptiness").
2. [You have returned them to me empty.]
3. Among those who go to Telmun,
4. who is the one who has treated me
5. in this way?
6. You show contempt to my messenger!
7. For the amount of silver
8. that you caused to go out from my hand (?),
9. you speak in this way.
10. And on your account
11. I myself gave 18 talents of copper
12. to the palace,
13. and Sumi-abum
14. gave 18 talents of copper.
15. Apart from what we left
16. as a sealed tablet at the temple of Shamash.
17. As for that copper,
18. since you have treated me (this way),
19. you have withheld my purse in enemy territory.
20. [You have withheld it.]
21. As for my purse, (to) my hand
22. in full (?) (there) is (a claim) of mine against you.
23. Know, therefore, that henceforth
24. I will not accept from you
25. copper that is not fine.
26. In my own courtyard
50. I will inspect (and) pick out each piece separately,
51. and I will take it,

**Left edge**

1. and because you have shown me contempt,
2. I will exercise rejection against you.

**Notes**

- The ATF numbering jumps from reverse line 26 to line 50. I translated the lines as given and did not restore any missing text between them. The sense is continuous, but this may be a numbering error in the transliteration.
- Obverse 17 is damaged and I could not translate it with confidence. The verb *tušīm* (obv. 17) is uncertain, as is its relation to *temešanni* (obv. 18).
- *tumeʾišam* (obv. 22), *tumeʾiš* (rev. 6) and *tumeʾišanni* (left edge) are rendered as "show contempt / treat with contempt". This rests on a conventional understanding of the verb, and the exact sense is uncertain.
- Obv. 19–rev. 2: the sense of "in enemy territory" (*ina qaqqar nakrim*) is unclear. It may be figurative, meaning that the messengers were treated as if on hostile ground.
- Rev. 8: *tušeṣṣûʾ* is translated literally ("you caused to go out"). The reference of this clause is unclear.
- Rev. 21–22: *šulmam elika išu* is very uncertain. I took it as "there is (a claim) against you".
- Rev. 50: *unassaq* is taken as "I will select / pick out".
- Left edge 2: *nasiḫtam epuška* is translated literally as "I will make a rejection against you". The exact legal sense is uncertain.
