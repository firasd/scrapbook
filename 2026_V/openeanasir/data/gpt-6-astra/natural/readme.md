### obverse

To Ea-nasir, from Nanni:

When you were here, you told me, “I will give Gimil-Sin good-quality copper ingots.” You went away, but you did not do what you had promised. You offered my messenger inferior ingots, saying, “If you want them, take them. If you [do not] want them, leave!”

Who do you think I am, [unreadable passage], to treat me with such contempt? I sent respectable men to someone like you to collect my money. Yet you humiliated them, and through hostile territory

### reverse

you sent them back to me empty-handed—not once, but twice. Who among those who travel to Dilmun has treated me this way? Yet you treat my messenger with contempt!

Is this how you speak to me over the [amount of silver you have taken from me]? I myself delivered eighteen talents of copper to the palace on your behalf, and Shumi-abum delivered eighteen talents, besides what is covered by the sealed document we left in Shamash’s temple. How have you treated me over that copper? You are holding on to my money in hostile territory. You are obliged to return it to me in full.

Understand this: I will not accept inferior copper from you here. In my own courtyard, I will pick out the ingots one by one and accept them.

### left edge

And because you have treated me with contempt, I will [reject unsuitable ingots].

### Notes

- The silver clause follows the supplied emendation *ma-<la>*, “the amount of.” The verb can refer either to silver already collected or to silver still to be collected from Nanni.
- The final *nasīḫtu* is interpreted here as rejection of ingots after selection; it may instead refer to a deduction charged against Ea-nasir.
