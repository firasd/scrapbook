### obverse

To Ea-nasir, from Nanni:

When you left, you told me, “I will give Gimil-Sin good-quality copper ingots.” You went away, but you did not do what you had promised. You set poor-quality ingots before my messenger and told him, “If you want them, take them. If you [do not] want them, go away!”

What do you take me for, that you [damaged words] and treat me with such contempt? I sent men of standing, men like yourself, to collect my money pouch, but you humiliated them, and from hostile territory

### reverse

you sent them back to me empty-handed, once and then again. Who among those who trade with Dilmun has ever treated me like this? Yet you have humiliated my messenger!

Is this how you speak to me after all the silver you have taken from me? On your behalf I gave the palace eighteen talents of copper, and Shumi-abum gave eighteen talents too, quite apart from the copper covered by the sealed document we deposited at Shamash’s temple. How have you treated me in return for that copper? You have held back my money pouch in hostile territory. You are responsible for getting it back into my hands intact.

Understand this: I will not accept poor-quality copper from you here. I will select the ingots one by one in my own courtyard and take them,

### left edge

and for the contempt you have shown me, I will [make a deduction against you].

**Note:** The final term, *nasīḫtum*, may refer to a deduction or to the rejection or selection of goods. The precise remedy Nanni threatens is uncertain.
