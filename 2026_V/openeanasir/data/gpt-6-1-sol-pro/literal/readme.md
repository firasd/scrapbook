## Obverse

Say to Ea-nasir: thus says Nanni:

When you came, you [said] to me as follows: “I will [give] good copper ingots to Gimil-Sin.” You went away, but you did not [do] what you [told] me. You [set] ingots that were not [good] before my messenger and said: “If you will take them, take them; if you will [not take] them, go away!”

What do you take me for, that [unreadable passage] you [treat me with contempt] like this? I sent men of standing to someone like you to collect my money, but you treated me with contempt and, in enemy territory,

## Reverse

once and twice you sent them back to me empty-handed. Among those who go to Dilmun, who has treated me like this? Yet you have treated my messenger with contempt!

You speak this way over the [amount of] silver you are to collect from me! And on your behalf I myself gave eighteen talents of copper to the palace, and Shumi-abum gave eighteen talents of copper, apart from that for which we left a sealed document at the temple of Shamash.

How have you treated me over that copper? You have held back my money in enemy territory. I have a claim against you for the return of my money, intact, into my hands.

You will learn that here I will not accept from you copper that is not good. [In] my courtyard I will select it one piece at a time and [take it].

## Left edge

And because you have treated me with contempt, I will reject your copper.

## Notes

- Brackets mark damaged but readable wording, supplied wording, or an unreadable passage. The damaged sequence in obverse 17 has not been reconstructed.
- “[Amount of]” follows the supplied correction **ma-<la>**; the syllable **la** is not written. This reading does not specify “one mina.”
- “Money” renders **kīsu**, literally “money bag” or “purse.”
- The final statement literally says, “I will make a rejection against you,” referring to rejection of the copper offered.
