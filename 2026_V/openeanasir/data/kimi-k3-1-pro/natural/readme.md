To Ea-nasir, from Nanni:

When you came, you told me, “I will give fine copper ingots to Gimil-Sin.” You left after making that promise, but you did not keep it. Instead, you set inferior ingots before my messenger and said, “If you want to take them, take them; if you [do not] want to take them, go away!”

Who do you take me for, that you […] and treat me with such contempt? I sent men of standing, comparable to yourself, to collect my money bag, but you slighted me and sent them back empty-handed through enemy territory, again and again. Who among those trading with Dilmun has treated me like this? You alone have humiliated my messenger.

reverse

Yet you speak like this over the silver you have obtained from me. On your behalf, I myself gave eighteen talents of copper to the palace, and Shumi-abum gave another eighteen talents, apart from the sealed document we both deposited in the temple of Shamash.

How have you repaid me for that copper? You have withheld my money bag in enemy territory. It is now your responsibility to return it to me intact. Understand that I will not accept copper from you here unless it is of good quality. I shall examine the ingots one by one in my own courtyard and take them myself.

left edge

And because you have treated me with contempt, I shall enforce my right of rejection against you.
