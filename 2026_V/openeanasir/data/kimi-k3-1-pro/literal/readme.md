**obverse**

Speak to Ea-nasir. Thus says Nanni:

Now, when you came, you spoke to me as follows: “I will give good copper ingots to Gimil-Sin.” You came and spoke to me thus, but you did not do so. You placed copper ingots that were not good before my messenger and said: “If you wish to take them, take them; if you [do not] wish to take them, go away!”

As for me, what do you take me for, […] that you treat me with such contempt? I sent men like yourself to collect my money, but you treated me with contempt: through enemy territory—

**reverse**

—you returned them to me empty-handed, one at a time and two at a time.

Who among those who travel to Telmun has treated me in this way? You have treated my messenger with contempt! You speak in this way concerning [all] the silver that you made me pay out. On your behalf, I myself gave eighteen talents of copper to the palace, and Shumi-abum gave eighteen talents of copper, apart from what we left in a sealed document for the temple of Shamash.

How have you treated me over that copper? You have held back my money in enemy territory. It is your responsibility to return my money to me in full. Know that I will not accept from you here any copper that is not good. In my own courtyard I shall select the ingots one by one and take them.

**left edge**

And because you have treated me with contempt, I shall exercise against you my right of rejection.

**Notes**

- Square brackets mark restored text; `[…]` marks an unreadable passage.
- Telmun is Dilmun, generally identified with Bahrain.
