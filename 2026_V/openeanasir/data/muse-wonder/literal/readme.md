obverse
To Ea-nasir speak:
Thus Nanni:
When you went, you said to me thus — thus you yourself:
“I will give good-quality copper ingots to Gimil-Sin.”
You went, and what you had said to me you did not do.
You set not-good copper ingots before my messenger, and thus you yourself:
“If you take them, take them; if you do [not] take them, go away!”
As like whom have you treated me, and [unreadable], that you scorn me thus?
I sent gentlemen like you to collect my (money-)bag,
but you scorned them: on enemy ground,
once and twice,

reverse
you sent them back to me empty-handed.
Among those who go to Telmun, who is there who has treated me thus?
You scorn my messenger, and concerning the full amount of silver that you caused to go out from my hand, you speak thus.
And for your sake, to the palace I myself gave 18 talents of copper, and Shumi-abum gave 18 talents of copper —
apart from what we left sealed for the temple of Shamash.
Concerning that copper, how have you treated me?
You have detained my (money-)bag on enemy ground.
You owe [the safe return of] my (money-)bag into my hand.
You shall learn that here I shall not accept from you copper that is not refined.
In my courtyard I shall select them one by one and take them.

left edge
And in return for your scorning me, I shall inflict a [removal(?)] on you.

Translation notes
- The partly unreadable sequence in obverse line 17 is left bracketed; no reading is restored.
- `[not]` in obverse line 15 restores the damaged negation in the transliteration.
- “Telmun” is Dilmun. “(Money-)bag” renders *kīsum*, which can denote a purse or the funds in it.
- The transliteration’s line numbering jumps from reverse line 26 to 50–51; the translation follows the supplied order.
