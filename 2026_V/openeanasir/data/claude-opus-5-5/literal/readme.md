**Obverse**

1. To Ea-nasir
2. speak:
3. thus (says) Nanni:
4. Now, when you came,
5. you spoke to me thus,
6. saying:
7. "Good ingots
8. I will give to Gimil-Sin."
9. You came, and you said (this) to me,
10. but you did not do (it).
11. Ingots that were not good
12. you set before my messenger,
13. and you said:
14. "If you will take (them), take (them);
15. if you will [not] take (them), go away!"
16. As for me, as whom
17. have you regarded me, that [...]
18. you treat me with contempt like this?
19. Gentlemen ("sons of men") like you
20. I sent to receive my money-bag,
21. (line 21 continues the previous line: "I sent")
22. but you treated (me) with contempt, and
23. in enemy territory

**Reverse**

1. once and twice
2. you sent them back to me empty-handed.
3. Among those who travel to Telmun,
4. who is there who has
5. treated me like this?
6. You treated my messenger with contempt.
7. Concerning the (full) amount of silver
8. that you took out of my hand,
9. you speak like this?
10. And on your behalf
11. to the palace
12. I myself gave 18 talents of copper,
13. and Šumi-abum
14. gave 18 talents of copper,
15. apart from that for which we left
16. a sealed document for the temple of Šamaš.
17. With regard to that copper,
18. how have you treated me?
19. My money-bag in enemy territory
20. you have withheld.
21. To restore my money-bag to my hand
22. in full is your obligation ("is upon you").
23. You will learn that here
24. copper that is not good
25. I will not accept from you.
26. In my courtyard
50. I will select (the ingots) one by one, and
51. I will take (them).

**Left edge**

1. And because you treated me with contempt,
2. I will exercise (the right of) rejection against you.

**Notes**

- Obv. 15: The ATF itself restores "[la]" ("not"). The sense requires it.
- Obv. 16–18: *tušīm* is taken as "you regarded/determined (me)," giving "as whom have you regarded me." Line 17 is damaged after *tušīmma* (*ni-x-(x)-x-x-ma*), and the broken word is not translated.
- Obv. 19: "Gentlemen" renders *mārī awīlī*, literally "sons of men," meaning men of standing.
- Rev. 2: *rīqūssunu* literally means "their emptiness," that is, (sent back) empty-handed.
- Rev. 3: Telmun is Dilmun, the Gulf trading centre. *ālik Telmun* means "those who go to Telmun," the Dilmun traders.
- Rev. 7: The ATF emends *ma-<la>* ("as much as, the full amount"). The phrase literally reads "for the full amount of silver which you made go out of my hand." The precise sense of the financial transaction is uncertain.
- Rev. 15–16: The syntax is compressed. *ēzib ša … kunukkam nīzibu* means literally "apart from that which … we left a sealed document." It probably refers to a separate, sealed transaction with the Šamaš temple.
- Rev. 21–22: *šullumam elīka išu* is literally "to make (it) complete is upon you."
- Rev. 26–50: The supplied line numbering jumps from 26 to 50. No missing text is marked in the ATF, and the sense runs on continuously ("In my courtyard I will select…"). The jump is likely a numbering irregularity rather than a gap, but this cannot be confirmed from the transliteration alone.
- Left edge 2: *nasīḫtam epuška* is literally "I will do/make a removal/rejection for/against you." It is understood here as rejecting the substandard goods. The exact legal nuance is uncertain.
