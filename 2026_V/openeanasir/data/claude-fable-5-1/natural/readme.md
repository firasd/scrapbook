**obverse**

Speak to Ea-nāṣir; thus says Nanni:

When you came, this is what you told me: "I will give good-quality ingots to Gimil-Sîn." You went off and said this to me—and then you did not do it. Instead you set ingots of poor quality before my messenger and said, "If you want to take them, take them; if you don't want to take them, be off with you."

Who do you take me for, that [...] you show me such disrespect? I sent gentlemen of standing—men like yourself—to collect my purse, and you treated them with contempt: in enemy territory,

**reverse**

once and then a second time, you sent them back to me empty-handed. Among those who trade with Dilmun, who has ever dealt with me like this? You alone hold my messenger in contempt. And on account of the silver you have had out of my hands, this is how you carry on!

Moreover, for your sake I myself paid eighteen talents of copper to the palace, and Šumi-abum paid another eighteen talents of copper—not counting the sealed document that the two of us deposited at the temple of Šamaš. And how have you treated me over that copper? You have kept my purse back from me in enemy territory. It is now your obligation to restore my purse to my hands in full.

Be advised that from now on I will not accept from you, here, any copper that is not of good quality. In my own courtyard I will pick over the pieces one by one and take [only those I choose].

**left edge**

And because you have treated me with such disrespect, I will exercise my right of rejection against you.

---

**Notes**

- Obv. 17: the second half of the line is damaged; whatever clause followed "Who do you take me for" cannot be read and is left unrestored.
- Rev. 7–8: the text has *ma-<la> kaspim*, emended by the editor; "silver you have had out of my hands" renders the relative clause literally. The phrase could instead refer to a specific sum owed, but the amount is not stated on the tablet.
- Rev. 26–28: "pick over ... one by one" renders *ištīnâ unassaq*, i.e. inspecting each ingot individually; the object "only those I choose" is implied, not written.
- Left edge 2: *nasiḫtam epēšum* is a technical expression, here understood as formally rejecting/returning substandard goods.
