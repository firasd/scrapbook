**Obverse**

1–3. To Ea-nāṣir speak: thus (says) Nanni.
4. Now, when you came,
5. you spoke to me as follows,
6. thus you (said):
7. "Good ingots
8. I will give to Gimil-Sîn."
9. You went (away), and (though) you had told me (this),
10. you did not do (it).
11. Ingots that were not good
12. you set before my messenger,
13. and thus you (said):
14. "If you (pl.) will take (them), take (them);
15. if you will [not] take (them), go away!"
16. For whom
17. do you take me, that ... [...]
18. and you treat me thus with contempt?
19. Gentlemen like yourself
20. I sent to receive my money-bag,
21–22. and you treated (them) with contempt, and
23. in enemy territory

**Reverse**

1. once and twice
2. you sent them back to me empty-handed.
3. Among those who travel to Tilmun,
4–5. who is there who has acted toward me in this way?
6. (Only) you treat my messenger with contempt.
7. On account of the full (amount of) silver
8. which you took out of my hand,
9. you talk like this.
10. Moreover, on your account
11. to the palace
12. I myself gave 18 talents of copper,
13. and Šumi-abum
14. gave 18 talents of copper,
15–16. apart from the sealed document which we deposited in the temple of Šamaš.
17. For that copper,
18. how you have treated me!
19. My money-bag in enemy territory
20. you have withheld.
21. (To return) my money-bag into my hand
22. in full — that is incumbent upon you.
23. (Know) that here
24. copper that is not good
25. I will not accept from you — learn (this)!
26. In my courtyard
50. I will pick (them) out one by one, and
51. I will take (them).

**Left edge**

1. And because you have treated me with contempt,
2. I will carry out a "removal" against you.

**Notes**

- Obv. 7, 11: *gubāru* here rendered "ingots" (lumps/bars of copper).
- Obv. 16–17: *tušīm-ma* taken as "you reckoned/regarded (me)"; the following *ni-x-(x)-x-x-ma* is damaged and left untranslated.
- Obv. 19: literally "sons of gentlemen like you."
- Rev. 3: Telmun = Tilmun/Dilmun.
- Rev. 7: *ma-<la>* restored by the editor; "the full amount of silver which you took out of my hand," i.e., which I paid you.
- Rev. 15–16: literally "apart from what, to the house of Šamaš, we left a seal(ed document)."
- Rev. 20: *tukti'il* from *kullu*, "to hold back, withhold."
- Rev. 22: *šulumam ... elika išu*, literally "it has (a claim) upon you (for) full (return)."
- Rev. 50–51: line numbers follow the ATF as given.
- Left edge 2: *nasiḫtam epuška*, literally "I will do a removal/picking-out against you"; the exact sense (rejection of substandard pieces, or expulsion) is uncertain.
